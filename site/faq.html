Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Consumer Directed Services and working with Devine Health.

CDS is a Missouri Medicaid program that lets eligible individuals stay in their own homes and direct their own care — including hiring, scheduling, and managing their own caregiver, who can often be a friend or family member.

Generally, you must be MO HealthNet (Medicaid) eligible, at least 18, and able to direct your own care or have someone who can direct it on your behalf. We’ll review your specific situation with you — just reach out and we’ll help determine eligibility.

Yes — that’s one of the biggest benefits of CDS. You can hire someone you already know and trust. Certain relatives (such as a spouse) may have restrictions, so we’ll help you confirm what’s allowed.

For eligible individuals, CDS is covered through Missouri Medicaid, so there is typically no out-of-pocket cost for covered services. We’ll explain exactly what’s covered before you enroll.

Devine Health manages payroll and timekeeping for your caregiver, so payment is handled reliably and on schedule. Caregivers simply record their hours — we take care of the rest.

We proudly serve the St. Louis, Missouri area. Not sure if you’re in our service area? Give us a call at (314) 221-3495 and we’ll let you know.

It’s easy: contact us for a free eligibility review, we’ll guide you through enrollment and paperwork, help you choose your caregiver, and then your personalized care plan begins. Get in touch to start.
