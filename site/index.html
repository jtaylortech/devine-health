Our Services

Care that meets you where you are

Flexible, in-home support designed around your needs, your routine, and the people you trust most.

Personal Care Assistance

Help with bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility, and daily activities — delivered with dignity and respect.

Homemaker Services

Light housekeeping, laundry, meal preparation, and errands that keep home comfortable and safe.

Respite Care

Dependable relief for family caregivers, so you can rest and recharge knowing your loved one is in good hands.

Why Choose Devine

Care you can trust, control you keep

With Consumer Directed Services, you direct your own care — and we handle the rest with professionalism and heart.

Licensed & Compliant

We follow Missouri CDS program standards so your care is safe, documented, and worry-free.

Compassionate Caregivers

Kind, dependable people who treat your family like their own — and often, they are family.

You Direct the Care

Personalized Care Plans

Every plan is built around your goals, health needs, and daily life — never one-size-fits-all.
