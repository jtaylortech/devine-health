About Devine Health

Care that keeps you in control.

We’re a St. Louis–based Consumer Directed Services provider, helping people live safely and independently at home — supported by caregivers they know and trust.

Our Story

Compassion is personal for us

Devine Health was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves to age and heal in the comfort of their own home, with dignity and independence intact. Consumer Directed Services (CDS) makes that possible — and we make CDS simple.

From your first phone call through enrollment and everyday care, our team walks alongside you. We handle the paperwork and the details so you can focus on what matters most: living well, at home, on your terms.

Led by Vernetta Collard, Devine Health brings warmth, professionalism, and a genuine commitment to the St. Louis families we serve.

What Is CDS?

Consumer Directed Services, explained

A Missouri Medicaid program that lets eligible individuals direct their own in-home care — including choosing their own caregiver.

You choose your caregiver

Hire someone you already trust — often a friend or family member — to provide your care.

You direct the care

You set the schedule and the tasks that fit your life, your health needs, and your routine.

We handle the rest

Enrollment, payroll, compliance, and support — we manage the program so you don’t have to.
